# EchoEarth — 5 Minute Demo Video Script

**Total time: 5:00**
**Format: Screen recording + voiceover. No face cam needed.**
**Tone: Calm, serious, not excited. Let the product speak.**

---

## BEFORE YOU RECORD

- Open the app at localhost:3000
- Pre-select Yamuna River, Today era (most dramatic numbers)
- Clear localStorage so journal and letters are empty (fresh demo)
- Ambient sound OFF at start
- Have a letter pre-typed but NOT sent yet (type it live during recording)
- Use a good microphone. Quiet room. Slow down your speech by 20%.

---

## SECTION 1 — THE HOOK (0:00 – 0:30)

**[SCREEN: Splash screen playing — "The glaciers are speaking... The rivers remember... Are you?"]**

*[Wait for all 4 lines to appear. Do not talk yet. Let the silence work.]*

**VOICEOVER (starts as "Are you?" appears):**
> "Every year, governments publish thousands of pages of environmental data.
> Nobody reads them.
> Not because people don't care —
> but because numbers don't make you feel anything."

*[2 second pause]*

> "EchoEarth changes that.
> We give rivers and lakes a voice.
> Their own voice.
> Grounded in real data."

*[Splash screen fades, app loads]*

## SECTION 2 — THE PROBLEM + CONTEXT (0:30 – 1:00)

**[SCREEN: Narration page has loaded. Yamuna River, Today. Don't interact yet — just show the page."]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "This is the Yamuna River. Delhi's lifeline.
> Health index: 19 out of 100. Critical."

*[Move mouse slowly to the health ring on the right panel. Hover on it.]*

> "Dissolved oxygen: 2.4 milligrams per litre.
> Fish need at least 5 to survive.
> The Yamuna has less than half of that."

*[1 second pause]*

> "These are real numbers. From the Central Pollution Control Board of India.
> But right now, they're being updated live —
> temperature from Open-Meteo's weather API,
> dissolved oxygen derived in real time using the Benson-Krause equation
> from APHA Standard Methods for water chemistry."

*[Gesture toward the green LIVE badges on the metrics]*

> "This isn't a static dashboard.
> This is the river, right now."

## SECTION 3 — THE CORE FEATURE: NARRATION (1:00 – 2:00)

**[SCREEN: Still on Yamuna, Today. Scroll up so the river name is visible."]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "But data alone isn't enough.
> So we asked: what if the river could speak for itself?"

*[Click the Play button. Let the TTS audio start playing.]*
*[Stay silent for 15-20 seconds. Let the river speak. This is your strongest moment.]*

*[The Yamuna's voice plays — something like "I am Yamuna, moving through a city that depends on me..."]*

**VOICEOVER (quietly, over the audio fading):**
> "That narration is generated fresh, every visit,
> by an AI that reads the actual live metrics —
> and writes in the river's voice."

*[Audio finishes or fade it. Show the narration text on screen.]*

> "Not a template. Not pre-written.
> The river speaks differently today than it did yesterday
> because today's data is different."

*[Scroll down slowly to show the Take Action section]*

> "And it doesn't just describe the problem.
> It ends with three specific things you can do.
> Concrete. Not 'reduce pollution.'
> Actually concrete."

*[1 second pause on the Take Action cards]*

## SECTION 6 — THE LETTER FEATURE (3:00 – 4:00)

**[SCREEN: Click back to Narration. Switch to Ganga, Today."]**

**VOICEOVER:**
> "Data makes you informed.
> But we wanted to make you feel connected.
> So we added something unusual."

*[Scroll down past the narration to the letter composer]*

> "You can write a letter to the river."

*[Start typing the pre-prepared letter — type it live, slowly, so it's visible]*

**[TYPE THIS LETTER — slowly, character by character:]**
```
Dear Ganga,

I grew up near your banks. My grandmother used to say you were the cleanest river in the world. I didn't believe her until I saw old photographs. I'm sorry for what we've done to you.

What can someone like me actually do?
```

*[Pause 1 second after finishing]*

> "And the river writes back."

*[Click Send Letter. Let the streaming reply begin.]*
*[Stay silent for 20 seconds while the reply streams in — this is your second strongest moment.]*

*[The Ganga replies — something personal, referencing the letter, ending with a specific ask]*

**VOICEOVER (as reply finishes):**

## RECORDING NOTES

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't rush. Every pause is intentional. The silence makes the dramatic moments land.
- Don't explain every button. Show, don't tell.
- Don't say "as you can see" or "so basically" — cut filler words completely.
- Don't show the code. Judges don't need it.

**What to do:**
- Record the audio separately from the screen if possible — cleaner.
- If your mic isn't great, use a free AI voice (ElevenLabs, Eleven Reader) to narrate the script. The irony of using ElevenLabs to narrate a project that uses ElevenLabs is worth mentioning.
- Add subtle background music — look for "ambient nature instrumental" on YouTube Audio Library. Keep it at 10% volume.
- The two silences (river speaking, river replying) are the emotional core. Do not cut them short.
